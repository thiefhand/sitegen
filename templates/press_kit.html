Description

Reclaimer is a first-person action-adventure set in a dystopian near-future world. You play as an unknown worker forced into the role of a prophesied hero after a pirate surgically installs a camcorder into your skull. Slash, zap, and grapple bio-mechanical robots as you uncover the secrets and plans of Null Corp, the evil corporation that owns everything. Destroy Null Corp, save the world, and reclaim your humanity!

Find clues and uncover a sprawling conspiracy gripping Beacon City. Dig up dirt on Null Corp. and use your conspiracy board to piece together the truth. Along with your fellow rejects of society, expose the evils of Null Corp. to the world and reclaim your humanity.

Wade through an onslaught of Null Corp.’s biomechanical workers, warriors, and constructed horrors tasked with stopping your crusade. Armed with your cutlass and deadly roller-boots, hack these foes to pieces and craft powerful weapons from their remains.

Meet strange characters, trawl forgotten passages, and break into secret facilities. Traverse the layers of the city to face Null Corp. head on. Get lost in the decaying world of Reclaimer and find beauty amongst the rubble.
